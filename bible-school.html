1. The Nature of Covenant

God relates to humanity through covenants — binding agreements sealed by His promise. From Noah to Jesus, each covenant reveals more of God's character and plan of redemption.

Key Passage: Genesis 9, Genesis 15, Exodus 19–24, Jeremiah 31:31–34, Luke 22:20

2. Faith of the Patriarchs

Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness. Study how faith walked before the Law and how it points us to Christ.

Key Passage: Genesis 12–22, Romans 4, Hebrews 11

3. The Law & the Promise

Moses received the Law at Sinai. Explore why the Law was given, what it reveals about sin, and how it leads us to the need for a Savior.

Key Passage: Exodus 20, Galatians 3, Romans 7–8

4. Prophets & Kings

Elijah confronted idolatry. David received an everlasting covenant. Solomon was given wisdom. See how God remained faithful even when His people were not.

Key Passage: 1 Kings 17–19, 2 Samuel 7, 1 Kings 3

5. The New Covenant in Jesus

Jesus inaugurated the New Covenant in His blood. It is written on hearts, offers full forgiveness, and brings the Holy Spirit to every believer.

Key Passage: Jeremiah 31:31–34, Luke 22:14–20, Hebrews 8–10

6. Living as Covenant People

How do we respond? Walk in faith, keep His Word, love one another, and share the good news of the New Covenant with the world.

Key Passage: Matthew 28:18–20, John 13:34–35, 2 Corinthians 5:17–21
