Holy Spirit Baptism

Community where children of God meet to talk about doing the good works of God, for the purpose of Salvation for each Individual by Faith in the Grace of the Lord Jesus Christ.

🕊️

Who We Are

We are a community of the children of God — gathered to encourage one another in the good works prepared for us, always pointing to salvation by faith in the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ.

✝️

Salvation by Faith

Not by works, so that no one can boast. It is by grace you have been saved, through faith — and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God (Ephesians 2:8–9).

🔥

Baptism in the Spirit

Jesus promised power when the Holy Spirit comes upon us, to be His witnesses (Acts 1:8). We gather to seek, receive, and walk in that empowerment for good works.
