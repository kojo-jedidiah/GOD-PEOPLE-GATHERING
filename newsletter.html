Covenant Newsletter

Stories of the covenants God made with His servants — from the flood to the cross

Old Covenant

Noah

The Rainbow Covenant

After the flood, God established an everlasting covenant with Noah, his descendants, and every living creature. The sign: the rainbow in the clouds. God promised never again to destroy all life with a flood.

Scripture: Genesis 9:8–17

“I establish my covenant with you: Never again will all life be destroyed by the waters of a flood.”

Old Covenant

Abraham

The Covenant of Promise

God called Abram and promised him land, descendants as numerous as the stars, and that through him all nations would be blessed. The sign of the covenant was circumcision. Abraham believed God, and it was credited to him as righteousness.

Scripture: Genesis 12, 15, 17

“I will make you into a great nation… and all peoples on earth will be blessed through you.”

Old Covenant

Moses

The Sinai Covenant (The Law)

At Mount Sinai, God gave Israel the Law through Moses. He declared them His treasured possession if they obeyed His voice. The covenant included the Ten Commandments and the sacrificial system that pointed forward to a greater sacrifice.

Scripture: Exodus 19–24, Deuteronomy 5

“Now if you obey me fully and keep my covenant, then out of all nations you will be my treasured possession.”

Prophetic

Elijah

The Prophet of Fire & Faithfulness

Though not the giver of a new national covenant, Elijah stood as God’s voice in a time of deep apostasy. On Mount Carmel he called Israel back to the covenant of the Lord. God answered with fire, and later took Elijah up to heaven in a whirlwind — a sign of divine approval and power.

Scripture: 1 Kings 17–19, 2 Kings 2

“How long will you waver between two opinions? If the LORD is God, follow him.”

Old Covenant

David

The Davidic Covenant

God promised David an everlasting dynasty: his throne would be established forever. This covenant finds its ultimate fulfillment in Jesus Christ, the Son of David, whose kingdom will never end.

Scripture: 2 Samuel 7, Psalm 89, Luke 1:32–33

“Your house and your kingdom will endure forever before me; your throne will be established forever.”

Wisdom

Solomon

The Covenant of Wisdom & Warning

God appeared to Solomon and offered him anything he desired. Solomon asked for wisdom to govern God’s people. God granted wisdom, riches, and honor — and warned that faithfulness to the covenant was required for the kingdom to endure.

Scripture: 1 Kings 3, 1 Kings 9:1–9

“I will give you a wise and discerning heart… If you walk in obedience to me… I will establish your royal throne.”

New Covenant

Jesus Christ

The New Covenant in His Blood

At the Last Supper, Jesus took the cup and said, “This cup is the new covenant in my blood, which is poured out for you.” The New Covenant, promised through Jeremiah, is fulfilled in Christ: the law written on hearts, full forgiveness of sins, and the indwelling of the Holy Spirit. No longer based on external obedience alone, but on the finished work of the cross and the resurrection.

Scripture: Jeremiah 31:31–34, Luke 22:20, Hebrews 8–10, 1 Corinthians 11:25

“This is my blood of the covenant, which is poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins.” — Matthew 26:28

All previous covenants find their yes and amen in Jesus. He is the better sacrifice, the better high priest, and the mediator of a better covenant.
