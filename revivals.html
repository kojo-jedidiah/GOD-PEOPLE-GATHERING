3 Days Prophetic Revivals coming soon

In every city near you, as we cover the world at large, for the Ministration of His Works to be heard, or seen even if not heard.

Thursday: Holy Spirit Service

(Teaching us how to ask) · 9am to 3pm

A day of teaching and seeking. Learn how to ask the Father for the Holy Spirit (Luke 11:13). Come ready to hear, pray, and receive instruction for the days ahead.

Friday: HolyGhost Baptism Day

(Any house of worship, in any city we gather as one) · 9am to 10pm

Baptism in the Holy Spirit — to be done at any house of worship, in any city we gather as one. Expect power to be witnesses (Acts 1:8) and the gifts of the Spirit.

Saturday: Gods People Gathering

(Recess and field activities — mostly music and sports) · 9am to 4pm

Celebrate as one body: recess, field activities, music and sports. Fellowship that strengthens the family of God and sends us out rejoicing.

For every week, in new cities and kingdoms on earth!
